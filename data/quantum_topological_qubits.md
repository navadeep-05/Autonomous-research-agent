# Quantum Computing & Topological Qubits: Architecture Guide

## Overview
Topological quantum computing relies on non-Abelian anyons and Majorana zero modes to protect quantum information from decoherence.

## Advantages & Mechanics
1. **Fault Tolerance**: Braiding operations are topologically protected, reducing physical error rates without massive surface-code overhead.
2. **Coherence Preservation**: Qubits store information non-locally across paired Majorana zero modes, preventing local environmental noise from corrupting quantum state phase.
3. **Hardware Implementations**: Nanowires coupled with s-wave superconductors under targeted magnetic fields are current leading experimental platforms.
